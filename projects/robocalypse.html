Robocalypse

A 1v1 dueling game set after the fall of mankind.

  • Global Game Jam 2022
  • Local 1v1
  • Team of 6

Robocalypse is a 1v1 dueling game set after the fall of mankind.

The goal of the game is to kill your opponent and be the first to reach the number of points set at the start of the game. Each kill is a point.

Weapons and shields

Each player has two weapons, an energy rifle and a normal rifle, and two shields that block the corresponding bullet.

Parrying

Blocking a shot with expert timing will result in a parry.

Parrying an energy shot will charge the next energy shot, making it phase through objects. Parrying a normal shot will deflect it.

Environmental hazards

Players should also look out for environmental attacks like meteors and exploding cars!

Play it

You're welcome to download the game from the Global Game Jam site. Two controllers connected to the PC are needed to play.
