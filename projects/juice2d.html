Juice2D

A lightweight, 2D-first game feel toolkit for Unity.

  • Unity 6 · URP 2D
  • Edit Mode previews
  • 70 presets

Juice2D is a lightweight, 2D-first game feel (“juice”) toolkit for Unity. The idea is simple: drop it in, click a preset, and your game feels better within 30 seconds.

It's made for indie and hobbyist 2D developers, game jam teams who need good-looking defaults fast, and programmers who want a tiny API like Juice.Shake(0.5f), while designers never have to touch code.

Why another juice toolkit?

Big all-in-one game feel frameworks already exist. Juice2D focuses on being 2D-first, with every effect tuned for sprites, orthographic cameras and the 2D Renderer; lightweight, with pooled objects and no per-frame allocations while effects run; great defaults, with presets that feel good immediately; and simple, with one static facade for code and components plus UnityEvents for designers.

Camera & time

Trauma-based camera shake that stacks naturally, with directional kicks and zoom punches. It's applied at render time, so it never fights your camera scripts or Cinemachine.

Hit stop and slow motion are stack-safe: freeze frames and smooth slow-mo ramps that respect your pause menu and never get stuck.

Screen effects

All screen effects share one render pass that costs nothing while idle: warp ripples with a chromatic, rainbow ring, anime-style impact frames, flashes and vignettes, chromatic aberration, desaturation, radial blur and glitch.

Sprite effects

Sprite effects work with any sprite and any material, and never modify the original renderer: hit flashes, afterimage trails, dissolves with a glowing edge, and outlines and glows.

Motion, particles & text

Frame-rate independent springs for squash & stretch and punches, procedural hit sparks with no art needed, and pooled damage numbers with crits, heals, misses and stacking combos.

Combos

Effects stack into sequences, and the 70 presets include combos like Parry, Bullet Time, Enemy Death and Boss Death.

Try before you apply

Every effect can be previewed in the editor without entering Play Mode, and the preview restores the scene exactly afterwards.

The Juice2D Studio window is a library of every effect and preset on a live example stage: audition it, tweak it, then save it as a preset or add it to a GameObject with one click.

Accessible by design

Global multipliers for shake, flashes, distortion and motion, plus a time-effects toggle, ready to wire into an options menu. Here's the same hit at master intensity 1, 0.5 and 0.

Built for real projects

Effects are non-destructive and stack-safe: they never overwrite your transforms, cameras or materials. In typical gameplay Juice2D costs about 0.04 ms of CPU per frame, and its render passes only run while an effect is active.

It runs on Unity 6 with URP (2D Renderer or Universal Renderer), ships with a playable demo scene, a PDF manual and full source code, and has a clean, XML-documented C# API: Juice.Shake(0.4f), Juice.HitStop(), Juice.Ripple(pos).
