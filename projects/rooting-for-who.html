Rooting For Who?!

A typing game about a group of cheerleaders rooting for The Ancient One.

  • Game Jam 2023
  • Theme: Roots
  • Typing game

The 2023 Game Jam's theme was “Roots”, and we decided to have some fun with it.

A group of excited cheerleaders are rooting for Y̷o̸u̸... but why can they travel through realms, and who are they really rooting for?

Rooting For Who?! is a typing game about a group of cheerleaders rooting for The Ancient One.

The game is endless and score-based. Typing words correctly increases your combo and adds to the meter; when the meter runs out, it's Game Over.

Successfully typing words will also slowly advance the cheerleaders to new spaces.

Be careful! Getting the same letter wrong 3 times will make the cheerleaders fall!

Difficulties

The game has 3 difficulties: Easy, Hard, which adds capital letters, and Very Hard, which adds special characters.
