The Race For Gamer Fuel

A 90s synthwave aesthetic endless runner inspired by Race The Sun.

  • Endless runner
  • Synthwave

The Race For Gamer Fuel is an endless runner set in a synthwave world.

It was inspired by this image from Google Images.

So what do you do other than go forward?

Going through pseudo-randomly placed rings in the world increases your speed and your score multiplier.

Dashing also gives some extra mobility.

Hitting an obstacle reduces health and clears some surrounding obstacles.

Picking up a health pack will... well, add health.

And if you're lucky, you'll find a golden ring which gives invulnerability.

Try and see how far you can get!
