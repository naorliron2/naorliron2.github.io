Heretic

A rework of the 1994 game Heretic, developed for the Doom Engine and published by id Software.

  • Final college project
  • Remake
  • First-person shooter

As the final project of our studies we were tasked with remaking a game from the 20th century, and we chose Heretic.

Heretic is a dark fantasy first-person shooter released in December 1994. It was developed by Raven Software using a modified version of the Doom Engine and published by id Software.

Weapons

The game has 2 weapons: a staff, which works as a low-damage machine gun, and a crossbow, which works as a high-damage, low-fire-rate weapon.

Melee

There is also a melee attack.

And if the enemy is stunned, a melee attack can be used to suck its soul, healing the player.

Levels

Our remake takes a lot of inspiration from the original game's level and monster design, while having some fun with the weapons and mechanics.
