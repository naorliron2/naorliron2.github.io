Milky Way Boba

Serve bubble tea to customers from all around the galaxy.

  • Game Jam 2025
  • Theme: Bubbles
  • Time management

The 2025 Game Jam's theme was “Bubbles”, and there was only one way this could go.

Tera, a little Earthian with big dreams, spent her childhood sipping bubble tea, gazing up at the sky, and imagining that one day she'd find her way among the stars. As time passed and technology advanced, Tera's dream slowly became reality. She finally found her place in the vast, infinite expanse of space, and in her hands she carried a piece of home: her beloved bubble tea.

Join Tera's daily life in her cosmic corner of the universe, as she serves bubble tea to customers from all around the galaxy!

Gameplay

The actual game revolves around serving customers their desired drinks by using the right dispensers in the right order.

Some recipes are harder than others. If you're not sure what to do, look at the recipe book!

Be careful: if you take too long or get the recipe confused, your customers will leave dissatisfied.

There are 4 recipes to make, and 3 cups to make them in.
