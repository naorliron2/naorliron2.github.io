Dam It!

A game jam game about a beaver that needs to fix his dam.

  • Game Jam 2020
  • Theme: Repair
  • My role: gameplay systems

The 2020 Game Jam's theme was “Repair”, and this is what we came up with.

You play as a beaver who needs to fix his dam by cutting trees in the surrounding forest.

Certain objects can be picked up, and all of them can be used to fix the dam.

Pesky humans will try to ruin your day.

A collection of collectible hats is also available.

And after some dam hard work, the dam can be fixed.

Gameplay systems were made by me; shaders, game design and art were made by my team.
