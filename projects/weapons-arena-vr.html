Weapons Arena VR

A VR game where the player has to destroy targets for score using an arsenal of weapons.

  • VR
  • OpenVR
  • First project

Since I bought a VR headset I wanted to play around with developing for VR, so I decided to make a few weapons you can throw, each with different behaviors.

Making throwing feel right

This was our first project, so I struggled a bit with making the game feel the way I wanted. Using OpenVR's built-in “Pick up” and “Throw” functions didn't give me what I was looking for.

So I tweaked the hold and throw (Velocity Estimator), and I'm pretty happy with how it turned out.

After changing the Rigidbody settings of the weapons, they each got their own feel.
