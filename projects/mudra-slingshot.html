Mudra Slingshot

A demo built to show the power of the Mudra Band for CES 2024.

  • CES 2024
  • Solo project
  • Wearable input

The Mudra Slingshot is a demo built to show the power of the Mudra Band for CES 2024.

The idea is simple: there's a slingshot which can fire at some targets.

Each player gets 10 shots with unlimited time, with score keeping!

At the convention

And of course, here is a clip of someone playing with the Mudra Band at the convention. Using the Wearable Devices Unity Plugin (which I created), we can get inputs from the Mudra Band in Unity.

This was a solo project by me, using models from the Unity Asset Store.
