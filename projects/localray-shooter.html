LocalRay Shooter

A demo created to showcase a shooter where one player has raytraced reflections.

  • Made at Adshir
  • Up to 4 players
  • Standalone · Android · iOS

The LocalRay Shooter was an interesting idea to showcase the company's raytracing capabilities: what if, in a shooter built around bouncing lasers, the player who scored the last kill has raytracing turned on, and all other players don't?

The project was made while I was employed by Adshir, but since I'm no longer an employee I don't have access to the raytracing engine. All reflective materials would have had raytracing in the original project.

The game supports up to 4 players and is cross-platform (Standalone, Android, iOS).

The game itself is pretty simple: each player can shoot, and the player who scores gets raytracing enabled. Most of the work was on the networking. There is also a heal in the middle of the arena.
