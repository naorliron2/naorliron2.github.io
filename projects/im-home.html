I'm Home

An endless bullet hell game in which your own father throws dad jokes at you until you collapse from an overload of cringe.

  • Game Jam 2024
  • Theme: Make Me Laugh
  • Bullet hell

The 2024 Game Jam's theme was “Make Me Laugh”, and the laughs were had.

I'm Home is an endless bullet hell game in which your own father throws dad jokes at you until you collapse from an overload of cringe.

Dad will try to make you laugh, and his punchlines will turn into deadly weapons.

And be careful, cringe is a powerful disease.

Be not afraid!

You have some skills yourself: dash with i-frames, and parry some attacks.
